The timetable used

Weekday schedule from exo's official train timetable data (GTFS), published 20 Nov 2026 and valid 19 Nov 2026 to 3 Jan 2027. No weekday service on 25 Dec and 1 Jan. Line 12 trains run Parc ↔ Bois-de-Boulogne in 4 to 9 minutes.

SAAQ service centre, 855 boul. Henri-Bourassa Ouest: weekdays 8:30 to 16:30, closed weekends. Times inside the SAAQ count from when you arrive or when the doors open, whichever is later. A trip that would keep you inside past 16:30 isn't offered.

"Waiting" adds up time outside the doors before opening and time on the platform. Ties go to the trip that gets you home sooner.
