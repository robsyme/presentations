Make one .bcm per chromosome with preprocess.py: see the guide.

How to read this. Enrichment shows only cells with clearly more shared barcodes than expected for their distance and coverage (z above 2); everything else stays blank. In O/E, red is more than expected and blue is fewer. Red blocks away from the diagonal are candidate misjoins or duplications; a red corner where two squares touch the diagonal suggests inversion breakpoints. Grey rows are low coverage. Hotspots tagged point sit on a single 5 kb row or column and are more likely a repeat copy than a breakpoint; hub N marks the top 10% of hotspots by the number of other hotspots sharing a 1 Mb window with them.
